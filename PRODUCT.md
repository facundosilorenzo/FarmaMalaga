# Product

## What this is
New website for **FarmaMálaga**, the three pharmacies of the Romero family in Málaga:

- **Farmacia Mata**, Calle Marqués de Larios 8, 29005 Málaga. Founded in 1880 in Plaza de la Constitución, on Calle Larios since 1894. Keeps the original furniture and *botamen* (apothecary jars) installed by its first owner, Agustín Pérez de Guzmán; listed in the Spanish Pharmaceutical Heritage inventory (Consejo General de Farmacéuticos). Owners: Agustín Pérez de Guzmán (to 1931), Antonio Mata Vergel, Antonio Romero Mata, today Pilar Romero García. Phone 952 211 915.
- **Farmacia García Carmona**, Calle Huéscar 2, 29007 Málaga, in the city's business district, open since 1976. Phone 952 391 549.
- **Farmacia Dr. Antonio Romero García**, Calle Eros 1, Benalmádena, next to Hospital Xanit Internacional. Phone 952 57 58 06.

Services: compounding lab (*formulación magistral*) at Farmacia Mata, with clean room and laminar-flow hood, authorized by the Junta de Andalucía to prepare for other pharmacies; homeopathy (reference pharmacy in the province, wide stock); herboristería opened in 1984 with its own entrance on Calle Marín García, behind Farmacia Mata. Own cosmetics line "FarmaMálaga" (sun care with aloe vera, rosehip oil, aftersun). Online shop at farmamalaga.com (PrestaShop, stays as the shop).

Contact: info@farmamalaga.com · WhatsApp +34 630 282 123.

## Audience
- Málaga locals choosing where to ask for advice or get a formula made, mostly on a phone.
- People who already know Farmacia Mata on Calle Larios and want hours, phone, or the shop.
- Tourists walking Calle Larios, curious about the historic pharmacy, needing sun care or a pharmacist.
- Other pharmacies and prescribers needing a compounding lab.

## Goals (in order)
1. Reach a pharmacist: WhatsApp, call, or walk in (which pharmacy, where, open now).
2. Send people to the online shop with intent (own line, sun care, dermo brands).
3. Make the services findable: formulación magistral, homeopatía, herboristería.
4. Build trust through 140+ years of real history on Calle Larios.

## Voice
Spanish from Spain, *tú*. Close, expert, calm, a pharmacist behind the counter: precise, never alarmist, no marketing superlatives. Health claims only as the products and services actually are.

## Surface mode
Persuade (impeccable): the home page must make clear in seconds who they are, that a pharmacist is one message away, and where the shop is.

## Constraints
- Static site, no build step, no dependencies. Content visible without JavaScript.
- Business data lives in `js/config.js`.
- Hours were taken from the old site (2020) and must be confirmed with the client before launch.
- No medicines are sold on this site; the shop keeps its own legal obligations (EU common logo for online medicine sales).
- No invented reviews, prices, awards or figures.

## Assumptions (inferred; the owner told us to proceed without an interview)
- The new site complements, not replaces, the PrestaShop shop.
- No real photography available yet; slots are prepared for the client's own photos.
